hey, so basically i moved into this new house and it's kind of old and small-town-ish. while we were unpacking i found this book under the bedframe in my room, it was all rusty under there. the book has this brown leather cover and it turns out its like an actual government diary from ages ago, apparently the government used to MAKE kids aged 10-18 write these by law which is honestly crazy to think about now.

i'm kind of into writing so this is actually really cool for me, like a real primary source i can use for a project. the diary belongs to someone called Angelos — that's not me, i just found his book. i'm going to try and put up entries as i read through it, i'm not doing it all in one go cause its a lot of pages and my handwriting-reading skills are not great lol.
